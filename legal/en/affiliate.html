1. Definitions

Program - the Monty affiliate program described at partner.monty.fast and in the Partner Dashboard.

Partner Dashboard - the area of partner.monty.fast where an Affiliate sees their code, statistics, and balance.

Affiliate Code - the unique code an Affiliate claims in the Dashboard. The same code forms both the Affiliate Link and the Promo Code.

Affiliate Link - a URL of the form monty.fast/r/<code>.

Promo Code - the same code in upper case, entered at checkout.

Referred Customer - a user attributed to an Affiliate under Section 4.

Creem - our payment processor, through which Qualifying Payments, Promo Code discounts, and Commission reconciliation are processed.

Qualifying Payment - a payment for a Monty plan actually received from a Referred Customer and not refunded, net of sales taxes (VAT, GST, sales tax and the like), Creem's published processing fee, and refunded amounts, after reconciliation. Commission is calculated on this amount, not on the list price.

Commission - the Affiliate's fee, calculated under Section 5.

Holding Period - the 30 calendar days following a Qualifying Payment (Section 6).

Payable Balance - Commission that has cleared the Holding Period and has not been reversed.

4. How a customer is attributed to you

4.1. Attribution lasts 90 days. A click on your Affiliate Link attributes that visitor to you for 90 calendar days from the click. If someone clicks today and pays three months later within that window, the customer is yours.

4.2. Last click wins. If a visitor clicks the links of several Affiliates within the attribution window, the customer is attributed to whoever was clicked last. Each new click restarts the 90-day count.

4.3. A Promo Code beats a click. If a Promo Code is entered at checkout, the customer is attributed to the owner of that code regardless of any earlier click. Entering the Promo Code also gives the Referred Customer a one-time 30% discount on their first payment only, through a single Creem discount code issued per Affiliate (a percentage discount, applied once).

4.4. A click on your Affiliate Link sets the attribution cookie immediately, with no cookie-consent gate. Attribution relies on that cookie and our server-side records. We cannot guarantee attribution where the visitor cleared cookies, switched device or browser, used private mode or a blocker, or where more than 90 days passed between the click and the payment. Losing attribution this way is not a breach of these Program Terms by us.

4.5. Lifetime, sealed at the first payment. Once a Referred Customer's first Qualifying Payment is received, their attribution to you is sealed: a later click on another Affiliate's Link, or entry of another Promo Code, does not move it. For as long as the Referred Customer stays attributed to you and keeps paying for Monty, Commission accrues on each of their Qualifying Payments, renewals included. If they stop paying, accrual pauses; if the same account resumes paying, accrual resumes.

4.6. Attribution ends if the customer was referred in breach of Section 10, and on termination of your participation under Section 11.

4.7. The click, sign-up, and payment figures in the Dashboard are informational. Where they differ from our billing records, our billing records govern.

6. Accrual, holding, and reversal

6.1. Commission accrues when a Qualifying Payment is received and appears in the Dashboard as held.

6.2. The Holding Period is 30 calendar days. After it, the Commission moves into your Payable Balance. The hold covers the window in which refunds, cancellations, and chargebacks can still happen.

6.3. Reversal. If a Qualifying Payment is refunded, cancelled, charged back, or found to be fraudulent, the matching Commission is cancelled. If it was still held, it simply never reaches your Payable Balance. If it was already paid out, the amount is deducted from your next payouts. If a charged-back payment's dispute is later won, the matching Commission is reinstated once an admin attests the outcome - Creem tells us only that a dispute was opened, never whether it was won or lost.

6.4. If a reversal exceeds your Payable Balance, the negative amount carries into later periods. We do not claim money back from you, except in cases of fraud or another breach of Section 10, where we may recover amounts already paid.

6.5. On a partial refund, Commission is recalculated pro rata on the amount we actually keep.

7.4. Payouts are made only in the USDC stablecoin, on the Base network or via BEP20, after reconciliation with Creem completes. You give us your payout address before your first payout. Until you do, your Payable Balance keeps accumulating and does not expire.
