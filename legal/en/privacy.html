5a. Google sign-in and YouTube API Services

Monty uses Google sign-in to authenticate you and YouTube API Services to connect your channel and upload videos you choose to publish. These are separate authorization steps. Signing in to Monty does not, by itself, give Monty permission to upload to YouTube. We do not ask for or store your Google or YouTube password.

Google sign-in data and purpose. With the openid, email and profile permissions, we receive your email address, its verification status and your name. We use these to verify your identity and create or sign you in to your Monty account. We store your email, name and sign-in provider with your account; the Google sign-in access token is used to retrieve this information during login.

YouTube permissions and data. When you choose to connect YouTube, Monty requests:

  • youtube.readonly — to retrieve your channel identifier, channel title and thumbnail URL and display the connected channel so you can check the destination before uploading. Although this permission permits broader read access, Monty's channel-connection feature uses it to retrieve this basic channel information, not subscriber lists, comments or analytics.
  • youtube.upload — to upload the video and publication details you select, such as its title, description and visibility. The publishing screen asks you to review and confirm the upload.

Storage and protection. We store the connected channel's identifier, title and thumbnail URL, granted permissions, token expiry information, and OAuth access and refresh tokens needed to keep the connection working. Connection tokens are encrypted in our database and are not returned to the browser's connection list. Authorized backend services and publishing workers use them to perform the requested upload. We protect data in transit with HTTPS and restrict access to the services and personnel that need it for the purposes described here. Publication records may include the uploaded video's identifier, link and status.

Sharing and AI processing. Our hosting and infrastructure providers process data needed to operate sign-in, storage and publishing. Google receives authentication requests and, when you request an upload, the selected video and its publication details. Media you provide for editing may be processed by the AI and media providers described in Section 5; YouTube channel metadata and OAuth tokens are not inputs to our video-generation models. We do not use Google or YouTube API data to train generalized AI or machine-learning models. Any sharing of Google API data is subject to the Limited Use restrictions below, including where the general sharing provisions in Section 5 would otherwise be broader.

Retention and automatic refresh or deletion. Account identity data is retained for your Monty account as described in Section 7. While your YouTube connection is active, Monty rechecks the authorization and the connected channel's information at least every 24 hours, and refreshes or deletes the stored YouTube API data described above (tokens, channel metadata, and upload results) no later than 30 calendar days after it was obtained. If a check finds the grant unusable, that same check starts removing its stored tokens, channel metadata and associated YouTube API publication results, retrying any incomplete cleanup until it finishes.

Disconnect. A Disconnect action in Monty revokes the authorization with Google immediately and, in the same action, deletes the stored connection — its tokens, channel metadata and YouTube API publication results. On the rare partial failure, the remaining cleanup is retried automatically by the next scheduled check, at most 24 hours later. Disconnecting Monty does not delete videos already uploaded to YouTube; you can manage those in YouTube Studio.

Revoking access through Google, and deletion requests. You can also revoke Monty's authorization directly in Google security settings, instead of disconnecting in Monty. Monty detects this on its next authorization check, at least every 24 hours, and deletes the stored YouTube data at that point — no separate deletion request is needed for that automatic cleanup. You can also ask Monty to delete your stored YouTube data at any time, either by using Disconnect or by writing to team@monty.fast; we delete it as soon as possible and within 7 calendar days. None of this affects data YouTube itself stores about your channel or uploads, which only YouTube controls.

Limited Use. Monty's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google API data is used to provide the features described here, not for advertising, sale to data brokers or building advertising profiles. Human access is limited to your affirmative consent, security or abuse investigations, or legal obligations, as allowed by that policy.

By connecting YouTube, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

5b. TikTok connection and Content Posting API

Monty connects your TikTok account so you can publish a finished video to it from Monty. Connecting TikTok is a separate authorization from signing in to Monty, and signing in does not by itself let Monty post anything. We never ask for or store your TikTok password.

Permissions and data. When you choose to connect TikTok, Monty requests:

  • user.info.basic — to receive your open ID, display name and avatar, shown on the publishing screen so you can see which account a post will go to.
  • video.publish — to post the video directly to your TikTok profile at your request.
  • video.upload — to send the video to your TikTok inbox as a draft instead, when you prefer to finish and post it in the TikTok app.

What we read when you publish. Immediately before the publishing screen is shown, and again when you press publish, Monty asks TikTok for your current creator settings: your nickname, the visibility options available to you, whether comments, duets and stitches are enabled on your account, and the maximum video length TikTok accepts from you. These are used to draw the screen and to stop a post TikTok would reject. They are not stored.

What we send. Only what you confirm on that screen: the video, its title, the visibility you selected, your comment, duet and stitch choices, and — if you declared it — whether the content promotes your own business or a third party. Nothing is sent to TikTok before you confirm.

Storage and protection. We store your TikTok open ID, handle and avatar URL, the granted permissions, token expiry information, and the OAuth access and refresh tokens needed to keep the connection working. Connection tokens are encrypted in our database and are not returned to the browser's connection list. Publication records may include the resulting post's identifier and status.

Sharing and AI processing. Our hosting and infrastructure providers process data needed to operate storage and publishing. TikTok receives the authorization request and, when you request a post, the selected video and its publication details. Media you provide for editing may be processed by the AI and media providers described in Section 5; TikTok account metadata and OAuth tokens are not inputs to our video-generation models. We do not use TikTok API data to train generalized AI or machine-learning models.

Retention, disconnect and revocation. Connection data is retained to maintain the TikTok connection you requested. A Disconnect action in Monty removes the stored connection, including its tokens, and Monty also asks TikTok to revoke the authorization, after which Monty disappears from your TikTok Manage app permissions. You can revoke it there yourself at any time. Disconnecting Monty does not delete posts already published to TikTok; you can manage those in the TikTok app. To delete your Monty account or other retained TikTok data, contact team@monty.fast.

While Monty's TikTok integration is under review, TikTok restricts posts made through it to private visibility, so a post published from Monty is visible only to you until that review completes.

By connecting TikTok, you agree to be bound by the TikTok Terms of Service. TikTok's handling of your data is described in the TikTok Privacy Policy.

Visitors who send a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal are only ever counted this way.
