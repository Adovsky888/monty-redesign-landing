Data Deletion Instructions

Effective date12 August 2026Last updated12 August 2026

You can delete your Monty account and the personal data associated with it at any time. This page explains every available path, including deletion of data received from connected social platforms (YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Telegram). Details on what we store and why are in the Privacy Policy.

1. Disconnect a social account

In the Monty app open Publish → Connected accounts and click Disconnect next to a platform. We immediately delete the access tokens for that platform and stop any access to it. Videos already published to your channel remain on the platform and under your control there.

2. Delete your whole account

Email team@monty.fast from the address you signed up with, subject "Delete my account". No form, no questions. We will:

1. Delete your account, profile and settings.
2. Delete your uploaded source clips and rendered videos from our storage.
3. Delete all social platform tokens and connection records.
4. Remove your email from the mailing list.

Deletion is completed within 30 days; we confirm by reply when it is done. Some minimal records we are legally required to keep (for example invoices, if you had paid for the service) are retained only as long as the law demands.

3. Mailing list only

Every email we send contains a one-click unsubscribe link. Unsubscribing removes you from all marketing email immediately.

4. Data received from platforms

If you connected a platform account (for example via Facebook/Instagram Login), you can also revoke Monty's access from that platform's own settings. Doing so invalidates our tokens; we delete the connection record and any platform-derived data on our side within 30 days of revocation or on your deletion request, whichever comes first.

5. Contact

Any question about deletion: team@monty.fast.
