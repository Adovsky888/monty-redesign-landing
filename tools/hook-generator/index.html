Video hook generator for Reels & TikTok

Type your video topic and get opening lines built from formulas that stop the scroll: questions, numbers, mistakes, stories. No AI call, nothing sent to a server.

Hook type

The formulas come from teardowns of viral short-form videos. The hook is the first second: drop in your topic, pick the line that fits your voice and rewrite it your way.

Type a topic and your hooks will appear here.

How it works

  1. Type your topic

    The more specific the better: not 'fitness' but 'morning workouts at home'.

  2. Pick a type

    Questions pull viewers in, numbers promise value, stories add emotion. 'All' mixes the formulas.

  3. Copy and rewrite

    Take the line and say it in your own words - a hook works when it sounds like you.

How to film the first three seconds

  1. Hook on the first word

    Skip the 'hey guys, today I'll talk about'. Open with the hook line and introduce yourself later, if at all.

  2. Put it on screen too

    Plenty of people scroll with the sound off. A big caption with the hook in the opening catches them as well.

  3. Close up, eyes on the lens

    Frame your face tight, look into the camera and say the hook like you mean it, as if talking to one person.

  4. Pay off the promise

    If the hook promises three mistakes, get to them fast. Viewers leave when the video doesn't deliver.

FAQ

What is a hook in a video?

A hook is the opening line or shot that decides whether a viewer stops or keeps scrolling. On Reels, TikTok and Shorts you get a couple of seconds for it.

Does this hook generator use AI?

No. It drops your topic into fixed formula templates: question, contrarian, numbers, mistake, story. It doesn't understand what your topic means, so treat the lines as starting points and adapt them to your video.

Is it free?

Yes. No sign-up, no payment, and you can generate as many times as you like.

Is my topic sent anywhere?

No. The lines are put together in your browser, and your topic text is not sent to a server.

How many hooks do I get?

Up to 8 lines per click, and 'Shuffle again' deals a new mix. With a single hook type you may get fewer, since each type has a limited set of templates.

How long should a hook be?

One short sentence you can say in a couple of seconds. If it doesn't fit in one breath, cut it down.
