The short version.

Is it really for life?
Yes. As long as the person you referred pays Monty, you get your rate - from 25% - on every payment. If they stop, accrual pauses. If they come back, it resumes.
When and how do I get paid?
On the 1st of each month once your balance is over $50. Each commission waits 30 days after the customer pays (the refund window) and then lands in your payable balance. We pay USDC to your wallet on Base or BEP-20: add the address in your cabinet once, no paperwork.
How long does my link last?
The cookie holds for 90 days. Someone can click today and pay two months later - the client still counts as yours.
Do I need ad experience?
No. If you have an audience - a blog, chat, channel - that's enough. You drop the link into content, Monty does the rest.
Which countries can join?
The program is global. Crypto payout solves it wherever a bank is awkward, including Russia and the CIS.
What's not allowed?
No paid ads on the Monty brand, no spam, and no promising people things the product doesn't do. Everything else is your field.
Is the rate always 25%?
25% is the start. Refer steadily and it climbs by tier: Silver 27%, Gold 29%, Platinum 30% on every payout. Your tier is set by active payments in the last 30 days - keep the pace, keep the tier. Every level and your progress are shown in your cabinet.
Can I earn from other partners?
Yes - that's the second level. Invite other creators as partners and you also earn up to +5% of every payment their own referrals make. We pay it on top, out of our side - it never cuts their share; together with their rate it caps at 32% of one payment. It doesn't stack deeper than two levels.
